Support

Training and support

Our founders spent years as franchisees, and the support program is built around what they wanted from a franchisor. Below is the assistance described in Item 11 of the Franchise Disclosure Document.

01

Site selection

Site selection and lease review assistance. Under the Site Selection Addendum you have 180 days to find a site, and we respond to each complete site submission within 30 days.

02

Design and build-out

A standard layout plan and design specifications at no charge, plus a written list of required equipment and suppliers and a suggested opening inventory.

03

Training

A 21-day program in the Denver metro area: 30 classroom hours and 124 on-the-job hours at our certified training location in Arvada. Training for up to four people is included.

04

Opening

We evaluate the restaurant before it opens. We may also send a representative and provide additional on-site help at opening, as we consider advisable.

05

Marketing

Help planning and running your Grand Opening Program (a minimum $20,000 spend you fund directly), plus brand marketing through the Marketing Fund.

06

Ongoing operations

Periodic field visits from an operations manager or Franchise Field Consultant, ongoing training, and access to the Brand Standards Manual.

Typical timeline: about 9 to 12 months from starting your site search to opening, depending on the site, lease, financing, permits, and build-out.
