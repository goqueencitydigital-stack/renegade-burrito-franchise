The Renegade Burrito story: built by franchise owners

Before they started Renegade Burrito, Brad and Tiffani Harris spent decades on the other side of the franchise relationship.

Years as franchise owners

Brad’s restaurant career began with Subway. According to an Arvada Chamber of Commerce member spotlight, he made his first franchise deal in 1996 with the help of a mentor, and over the years he and Tiffani grew a portfolio that the Chamber describes as 35 Subway restaurants plus 15 other franchises across seven brands. Renegade Burrito’s FDD puts it at 50 restaurants across 7 national and regional franchise brands over 30 years.

That experience shaped how they think about operations, training, and what franchise owners need from a franchisor.

Starting their own brand

In 2017 they opened the first Renegade Burrito in Westminster, Colorado (Renegade Burrito). The idea was straightforward: made-to-order burritos, bowls, and tacos, with breakfast burritos served all day and food made fresh in-house.

The brand grew across the Denver area, including an Arvada restaurant that opened in 2021 and a stand inside Empower Field at Mile High (Arvada Chamber; What Now Denver). In October 2026, Westword reported the opening of the Lakewood location at 220 Union Boulevard as the brand’s sixth.

Westword readers named Renegade Burrito the 2025 Readers’ Choice for Best Burrito and Best Breakfast Burrito (Westword).

Opening the door to franchise partners

Renegade Burrito Development LLC was formed in 2022 to offer Renegade Burrito franchises. The training program is led by Tiffani Harris and Training Director Erika Pazol, and franchise development is led by Dan Demolli.
