Our team

Meet the team

Renegade Burrito is led by people who have run restaurants themselves. Here's who you'll work with as a franchise owner.

BH

Brad Harris

Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Brad began his restaurant career with Subway and made his first franchise deal in 1996. Over 30 years, he has owned and operated 50 restaurants across 7 national and regional franchise brands, including through North Peak Ventures, his restaurant franchise investment company. He has served on several franchise advertising and marketing boards and committees, and he co-founded Renegade Burrito with Tiffani in 2017. Brad holds a bachelor's degree from the University of Northern Colorado.

TH

Tiffani Harris

Co-Founder and Chief Marketing Officer

Tiffani co-founded Renegade Burrito in 2017 and leads the brand's marketing. She also leads classroom training for new franchise owners alongside Training Director Erika Pazol.

DD

Dan Demolli

Executive Vice President of Development

Dan leads franchise development, working with prospective owners from the first conversation through signing.

RB

Rod Boyer

Executive Vice President of Operations

Rod leads restaurant operations, the standards and systems franchise owners run every day.

EP

Erika Pazol

Training Director

Erika leads the initial training program with Tiffani Harris, preparing new owners and managers before opening.
