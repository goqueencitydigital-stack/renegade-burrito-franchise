Franchise opportunities in Denver

Colorado is Renegade Burrito's home market and its first priority for franchise development. Guests here already know the brand.

Where the brand has grown

Renegade Burrito opened in Westminster in 2017 and has grown across the Denver metro area.

Why start in Colorado

Training happens at the Arvada restaurant, the leadership team is local, and Westword readers named Renegade Burrito Best Burrito and Best Breakfast Burrito in 2025. Specific territories are confirmed during the discovery process.
