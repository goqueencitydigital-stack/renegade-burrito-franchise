The process

From inquiry to opening

Eight steps, typically 9 to 12 months from site search to opening day. Here's what to expect.

  1. Submit an inquiry

    Tell us about yourself, your market, and your timeline. It takes about four minutes.

  2. Introductory call

    A member of our franchise development team calls to learn about your goals and answer your first questions.

  3. Receive and review the FDD

    If there's a mutual fit, you receive our Franchise Disclosure Document. Federal law requires at least 14 calendar days between receiving it and signing any agreement or paying any money. Review it with a franchise attorney and an accountant.

  4. Discovery Day

    Meet the leadership team, visit restaurants, and spend time in the kitchen. Both sides decide whether the partnership is right.

  5. Sign the franchise agreement

    The $35,000 initial franchise fee is paid when you sign the franchise agreement.

  6. Find and approve your site

    You have 180 days after signing to find a site, and we respond to each complete site submission within 30 days. We help with site evaluation and lease review.

  7. Train

    Your Operating Owner and General Manager complete our 21-day initial training program in the Denver metro area: 30 classroom hours and 124 on-the-job hours. Up to four people are included.

  8. Open your restaurant

    Most restaurants open about 9 to 12 months after the site search begins, depending on the lease, financing, permits, and build-out.
