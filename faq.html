FAQs

Franchise questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Submit an inquiry and we'll follow up.

Does Renegade Burrito offer franchises?

Yes. Renegade Burrito Development LLC offers franchises for Renegade Burrito restaurants, as well as Area Development Agreements for multiple restaurants. Submit a short inquiry to start the conversation.

How much does a Renegade Burrito franchise cost?

The initial franchise fee is $35,000. The estimated initial investment for a standard restaurant is $389,500 to $831,000, including the franchise fee.

What are the ongoing fees?

A royalty of 5% of gross sales and a marketing contribution of 4% of gross sales, both paid weekly. Other fees are listed in Item 6 of the FDD.

Do you share sales or earnings information?

No. Renegade Burrito does not make financial performance representations (Item 19), and our team is not authorized to make any earnings claims, orally or in writing.

Do I need restaurant experience?

Restaurant or multi-unit leadership experience is strongly preferred. Your Operating Owner and General Manager must complete our initial training program before opening.

What formats are available?

Standard restaurants are about 1,600 to 2,400 sq ft, typically in strip centers or stand-alone buildings. Non-traditional formats may also be offered.

Do I get a protected territory?

Each franchise includes a Protected Area, typically a 3-mile radius from your restaurant's front door. It is not an exclusive territory; see Item 12 for the details and exceptions.

How long is the franchise term?

The earlier of 10 years from opening or 11.5 years from signing, with two additional 5-year renewal terms if you meet the renewal requirements.

Which markets are you considering?

We are starting with Colorado and the Mountain West. See the Available Markets page for current status.

Can I open more than one location?

Yes. Area Development Agreements cover a minimum of three restaurants, developed on an agreed schedule.

What training is provided?

A 21-day program in the Denver metro area with 30 classroom hours and 124 on-the-job hours at our certified training location in Arvada. Training for up to four people is included.

How long does it take to open?

About 9 to 12 months from starting your site search to opening, depending on the site, lease, financing, permits, and build-out.

When will I receive the FDD?

Qualified candidates receive the FDD during the discovery process. Federal rules require at least 14 calendar days between receiving the FDD and signing an agreement or paying any money.

Is financing available?

Renegade Burrito does not offer direct or indirect financing (Item 10). Many franchisees work with lenders familiar with restaurant franchises.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

A member of our franchise team will follow up to schedule an introductory call.
