15 questions to ask before buying a restaurant franchise

Choosing a restaurant franchise is part research project, part relationship. The right questions help you compare brands fairly and spot gaps before you commit. Here are 15 to bring to every franchisor conversation.

About the brand

  1. How long has the brand been operating, and how many locations are open today? Ask how many are company-owned and how many are franchised.
  2. Who leads the company, and what operating experience do they have? Look for leaders who have run restaurants themselves.
  3. What makes the menu different? Daypart, signature items, and preparation methods all affect labor and sales patterns.

About the investment

  1. What is the estimated initial investment, and what does it include? This appears in Item 7 of the FDD. For Renegade Burrito, the estimate is $389,500 to $831,000 for a standard restaurant, including the $35,000 franchise fee.
  2. What are the ongoing fees? Royalties, marketing or brand-fund contributions, and technology fees appear in Item 6.
  3. Does the franchisor make a financial performance representation? If so, it appears in Item 19 of the FDD. Franchisors may only share financial performance information through Item 19 — be cautious of any figures offered informally.

About support

  1. What does initial training cover, how long does it last, and where does it happen?
  2. What support is provided at opening?
  3. How does ongoing field support work?
  4. What marketing support is provided at the local and brand level?

About territory and growth

  1. Is a protected territory offered, and how is it defined? See Item 12.
  2. What are the requirements to open additional units?

About the relationship

  1. Can I speak with current and former franchisees? The FDD lists them in Item 20.
  2. Has the franchisor been involved in litigation or bankruptcy? See Items 3 and 4.
  3. What happens at renewal, transfer, or if I want to exit? See Item 17.

Take the next step
