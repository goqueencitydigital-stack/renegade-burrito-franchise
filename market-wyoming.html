Wyoming

Wyoming restaurant franchise opportunities

Renegade Burrito is evaluating Wyoming for future franchise development. If you're an experienced operator in Wyoming, we'd like to hear from you.

Why Wyoming

Wyoming shares Colorado's northern border, so it's a natural next step for a Colorado-born brand. Cheyenne and Laramie sit along the Front Range corridor that already knows Renegade Burrito.

Communities we'd like to hear about

Cheyenne, Casper, Laramie, and Gillette, along with other Wyoming communities where an operator has strong local ties.

What we're looking for in Wyoming

  • Experienced restaurant or multi-unit operators
  • Owner-operators, or investors with a committed operating partner
  • Interest in one restaurant or an Area Development Agreement for three or more
  • Strong ties to the community you want to serve

Specific territories are confirmed only during the discovery process, and franchise offers begin after the Franchise Disclosure Document is issued.
