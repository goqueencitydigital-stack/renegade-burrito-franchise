Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026.

This policy explains how Renegade Burrito Development LLC ("Renegade Burrito," "we," "us") handles information collected through this franchise website. It does not cover the restaurant ordering website at renegadeburrito.com.

Information you give us

When you request information, ask for the brochure or apply, we collect what you enter: your name, email, phone, home state, the market you're interested in, experience, timeline, available capital range, notes, and your contact preferences.

Information collected automatically

We record how you arrived at the site (for example, campaign tags such as utm_source, or ad click IDs) and the first page you visited. This is kept in your browser for the current session only and is sent with your inquiry. Fit quiz answers stay in your browser unless you choose to submit an inquiry.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and send franchise information you asked for.
  • To evaluate whether a franchise may be a fit, and to deliver the Franchise Disclosure Document when appropriate.
  • To understand which marketing brings in inquiries.
  • To contact you by phone, text or email only in the ways you agreed to. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or ask us to stop at any time.

Who receives it

Inquiries are delivered by email to Renegade Burrito's franchise team and its marketing agency, Queen City Digital, through a form-delivery service. We don't sell your information. We may share it with service providers who help us run the franchise program, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records as long as needed to work with you and to meet franchise-law record-keeping requirements, then delete or anonymize them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or opt out of marketing, by emailing bharris@renegadeburrito.com or calling (720) 218-9261.

Children

This site is for adults considering a business investment. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We'll post any updates on this page with a new "last updated" date.
