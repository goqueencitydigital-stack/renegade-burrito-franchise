Investment

Franchise investment

The figures below come from Renegade Burrito's Franchise Disclosure Document. Review the full FDD with a franchise attorney and an accountant before making any decision.

Initial franchise fee$35,000, paid at signing
Estimated initial investment: standard restaurant$389,500 to $831,000, including the franchise fee
Royalty5% of gross sales, paid weekly
Marketing contribution4% of gross sales, split between the Marketing Fund and local marketing
Grand Opening ProgramAt least $20,000, spent by the franchisee
Initial trainingUp to four people included; $500 for each additional person
TechnologyPOS and computer system $2,500 to $7,500 at opening
Multi-unit developmentArea Development Agreements for a minimum of three restaurants
FinancingNot offered by the franchisor
Financial performance representationsNone. Renegade Burrito does not make financial performance representations.

What's included

Understanding your investment

Item 7 of the FDD lists each category with a low and high estimate, including the initial franchise fee, lease, construction, equipment, signage, opening advertising, inventory, training travel, professional fees, and three months of additional funds.

Ongoing fees, including royalty and marketing contributions, are listed in Item 6.
