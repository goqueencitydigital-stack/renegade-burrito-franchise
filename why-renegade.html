Why Renegade

Not another cookie-cutter burrito chain

Renegade Burrito was started by people who know franchising from the inside, and who wanted to build a brand they'd be proud to run.

Our story

From franchisees to founders

Brad Harris began his restaurant career with Subway and made his first franchise deal in 1996. Over 30 years he has owned and operated 50 restaurants across 7 national and regional franchise brands, including through North Peak Ventures, his restaurant franchise investment company.

In 2017 they opened the first Renegade Burrito in Westminster, Colorado. Today the company operates restaurants in Thornton and Arvada and kitchens in Denver's DU and Park Hill neighborhoods, alongside licensed locations, a stand inside Empower Field at Mile High, and a Lakewood restaurant that opened in October 2026.

What sets us apart

The Renegade difference

Breakfast all day

Breakfast burritos aren't limited to the morning. They're on the menu from open to close.

Made fresh in-house

Salsas and ingredients prepared daily in the restaurant.

Recognized by Denver diners

Westword readers named Renegade Burrito Best Burrito and Best Breakfast Burrito in the 2025 Readers' Choice awards.

More than one way to open

Franchises are offered for standard restaurants, and the franchisor may also offer non-traditional formats with their own investment estimate.

Who we're looking for

The Renegade franchise partner

  • Experience leading restaurant teams or multi-unit operations
  • Hands-on involvement in the business, or a committed operating partner
  • Care for food quality and guest experience
  • Financial readiness for the investment disclosed in Item 7 of the FDD
  • An Operating Owner and General Manager ready to complete the 21-day initial training program
  • Ties to the community you want to serve
